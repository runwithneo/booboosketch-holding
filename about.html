Booboosketch is the practice of Ben Heller.

Site-specific visual systems for architectural-scale environments. The work is hard-edge, restraint-first, and designed to read at scale — closer in register to architectural collaboration than traditional muralism.

Recent and ongoing projects span civic, commercial, transit, and educational environments — including Brightline, 5th Third Bank, FAU Research Park, and Bridge Prep Academy.
